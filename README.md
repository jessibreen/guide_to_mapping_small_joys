# A Guide to Mapping Small Joys

A classroom exercise in which students mark the places on campus where small things make them happy, then compare maps to see how differently people experience the same place. The conversation that follows moves from shared joys toward questions about bodies, identity, belonging, and whose experiences maps leave out.

Created by Jessica Breen, Geospatial Research Lab, American University.

## Running the exercise

Plan for about 75 minutes, with at least half of that for discussion.

1. Print one zine and one campus map per student (11 x 17 in works best for the map), plus a mix of drawing supplies.
2. Students read the zine, then mark their own small joys on the map however they like.
3. Students share in pairs, then the whole group discusses, following the stages in the facilitation guide.
4. Close with a one-sentence reflection from each student.

The facilitation guide has the full details.
